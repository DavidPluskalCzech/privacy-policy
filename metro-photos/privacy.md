## Overview
Metro Photos is a photo gallery app designed to provide a simple way to browse your photo library. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Metro Photos accesses and handles your data.

## Photo Library
Metro Photos requires access to your photo library to display your photos, videos, albums, and other library content. 
Your photo library is accessed locally on your device using Apple’s Photo Library APIs. Metro Photos does not upload your photos or videos to our servers or transmit them to third parties.
If you choose to grant limited access to your photo library, Metro Photos can only access the photos and videos you have selected.

## Purchase Information
In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information.
Metro Photos may check the status of your purchase to determine whether the full version of the app has been unlocked.

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- Metro Photos does not collect analytics or usage data.
- We do not track your location.
- We do not upload or collect your photos or videos.
- We do not use your photo library for advertising or tracking.
- We do not share your personal data with third parties.
- We do not use advertising frameworks.

## Photo Library Permission
You can change Metro Photos’ access to your photo library at any time in iOS Settings.
If you revoke access, Metro Photos will no longer be able to display the affected content from your photo library.
