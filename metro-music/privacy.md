# Metro Music – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

## Overview
Metro Music is a music player for the audio files you import and, if you choose to connect it, your Apple Music library. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what Metro Music accesses, what it stores, and where your data goes.

In short: **Metro Music does not collect your data.** It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own.

## Your music files
You choose audio files with the iOS Files picker. Metro Music copies them into its own private storage on your device, reads their tags (title, artist, album, track and disc number, year, genre, cover art and duration) and keeps that information in a library database on your device.

Your files and their tags stay on your device. Metro Music does not upload them to us or to anyone else.

## Apple Music (optional)
If you allow Metro Music to access Apple Music (the “Media & Apple Music” permission), it can read your Apple Music library, playlists and “loved” ratings, search the Apple Music catalog, and play songs through Apple’s MusicKit.

This happens directly between your device and Apple. Metro Music’s developer does not receive any of it. When you favorite a song, add a song to your library, or create or add to a playlist in Metro Music, that change is sent to your Apple Music account as an action you took. Artwork is downloaded from Apple’s servers. Apple’s handling of this data is described in [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

You can change Metro Music’s access to Apple Music at any time in iOS Settings. Without it, Metro Music still plays the files you imported.

## What Metro Music stores on your device
- Your library, playlists, favorites, last-played times and hidden items, and your app settings (such as the accent color).
- A small snapshot of what is playing and what you played recently, shared between the app and its home screen widget through an App Group, so the widget can show it. It never leaves your device.
- Trial information. To apply the free trial, Metro Music stores the date you first used the app and the last date it saw, in the app’s settings and in the iOS Keychain. This is not sent anywhere. Because an item in the Keychain can remain after an app is deleted, a reinstalled app still knows when the trial started.

This data stays on your device. It may be included in the device backups you make with iCloud or a computer, according to your own backup settings.

## Purchase information
The full version is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information. Metro Music only asks Apple whether the purchase is active on your Apple Account, to decide whether the full version is unlocked.

## Data we do NOT collect
- Metro Music does not collect analytics or usage data.
- We do not use third-party SDKs for analytics, crash reporting or advertising.
- We do not track you, and we do not use advertising frameworks or identifiers.
- We do not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
- We do not upload your music files, library or playlists.
- We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

## Diagnostics from Apple
If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We cannot identify you from them.

## Children
Metro Music is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Your choices and deleting your data
- Removing a song inside Metro Music deletes the app’s copy of that file and its library entry. It does not touch the original file you imported.
- Deleting Metro Music removes your library, playlists, settings and the copies of your files from your device. The trial dates kept in the Keychain may remain, as explained above.
- Your Apple Music data belongs to your Apple Account and is managed in Apple Music and in iOS Settings.
